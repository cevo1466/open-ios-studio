# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Open iOS Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone who takes part, regardless of experience, background, identity or location. We pledge to keep participation free of harassment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Be honest about what is tested and what is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through a private channel: use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for anything sensitive, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately where possible.
